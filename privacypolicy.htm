Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

What we collect

  • Untappd account details when you sign in: username, name, avatar, location and an access token, so the app can search Untappd for you.
  • Your Cellar data: beers, quantities, prices, dates, notes, sizes, photos, Collections and Wish List.
  • Basic usage data when the app starts or resumes: a random device ID, platform, app version, device model and OS version.
  • Server logs (such as IP address and requests) kept by our hosting provider to run and troubleshoot the service.

How we use it

Only to run Cellar: storing your inventory, showing it back to you, and understanding how the app is used so we can improve it. We don't show ads, and we don't sell or share your information with third parties.

Sharing

When you share your Cellar or a Collection, anyone with the link can see those beers, notes and photos. Resetting your Cellar token in the app turns off old Cellar links.

Children

Cellar is a beer app intended for adults of legal drinking age and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in the app from Menu → Delete Account. This permanently removes your Cellar, Collections, Wish List, notes and photos. Your Untappd account isn't affected.
